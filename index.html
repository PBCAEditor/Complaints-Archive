What this site is

The Peabody Complaints Archive is a website documenting complaints, disputes and resident experiences involving Peabody Trust and associated managing organisations. The name is used with a degree of irony: the site exists precisely because residents have found the ordinary complaints process an inadequate route to accountability.

The Peabody Complaints Archive (PCA) is written and run by leaseholders and residents, including members of the Residents' Association. It is independent of Peabody Trust: not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Peabody or any of its managing agents. It does not claim to be a neutral or disinterested party. It is a resident's account, openly written from the resident's side, and held to a standard of documentary accuracy.

Peabody Trust and anyone else referred to here is welcome to reply. See corrections and right of reply, or read more about this site.

    Are you a Peabody resident? Get in touch

    If you are a Peabody leaseholder, shared owner or tenant and you have had similar experiences with service charges, complaints, repairs or transparency, this site would like to hear from you. Documents, correspondence and first-hand accounts all help build a fuller picture.

    We also welcome responses to anything published here, including from Peabody itself and anyone else named. Substantive responses and supporting evidence will be considered in accordance with the site's corrections and right-of-reply policy. Submissions are reviewed before anything is published, and nothing is posted automatically or unmoderated.

    Write to PBCAEditor@gmail.com. Before sending documents, please read sending information and evidence safely. See also the corrections and right of reply and privacy pages for how submissions are handled.
