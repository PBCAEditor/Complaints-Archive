Privacy

Last updated: August 2026

The site may receive personal information from people who contact it or submit information for editorial review. This may include names, email addresses, and documents or correspondence relating to housing issues.

Information received is normally used only to review submissions, communicate with contributors, or verify information used in articles.

Where documents are published, personal information may be redacted where appropriate. Residents who provide information may request anonymity, and the site will normally respect that unless publishing an identifying detail is necessary for accuracy or verification.

The site does not sell personal information and does not share personal data with third parties except where required by law.

This site uses privacy-friendly analytics (GoatCounter) to count how many people visit and which pages they read. These analytics do not use cookies and do not collect information that identifies individual visitors.

Anyone who has provided personal information may request that it be deleted, subject to any legal or editorial requirement to retain it.

This site is written and run by leaseholders and residents, including members of the Residents' Association. It is independent of Peabody Trust and is not operated by or on behalf of Peabody Trust.
